# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in FlyThe BG welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect project boundaries, privacy, and intellectual property.
- Report security or privacy issues privately rather than publishing sensitive details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, sexual harassment, or other conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or unwelcome is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent loss of participation privileges.

## Reporting

Please report conduct concerns to **stackpilotfe@outlook.com** with the subject line `Code of Conduct`. For security vulnerabilities or sensitive privacy issues, do not include exploitable details in a public issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing or participating in the FlyThe BG project.
