# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in FlyThe BG and its open-source community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or any other personal characteristic.

By participating in this project, you agree to help create a positive and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a healthy community include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Using inclusive language and avoiding personal attacks or discriminatory remarks.
- Respecting project maintainers, contributors, users, and community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in FlyThe BG spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, and other project-related communication:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language or conduct.
- Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or other inappropriate conduct.
- Publishing private or personally identifying information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Spam, malicious content, or behavior intended to harm the project or its users.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make another community member feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository. Please do not create a public issue for sensitive reports.

When reporting an incident, include as much relevant information as you can, such as what happened, where it happened, when it happened, and any available links or evidence. Please avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

The maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible and will respect confidentiality to the extent practical.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction of participation, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will be made with the goal of protecting the community and maintaining a respectful environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official FlyThe BG project spaces and when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the FlyThe BG project.

## Questions

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, please contact the FlyThe BG project maintainers through the repository's available contact channels.
